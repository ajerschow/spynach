# EasySpin fixtures

Reference data for the `easyspyn` parity tests, written by EasySpin 6.0.15 in
MATLAB R2026b on the Mac through `oracle/run_easyspin_fixtures.sh`
(`oracle/matlab/easyspin/run_easyspin_fixtures.m`, settings block at the top).
Fixtures live at `/Users/alexej/Documents/PROG/easyspin-fixtures/6.0.15/<case>/`
(`$EASYSPYN_FIXTURES` for the tests), separate from the Spinach fixtures. The
installed EasySpin release is only read.

Every `pepper`, `garlic`, `salt`, `curry`, `esfit`, `chili` and `saffron` case
written since 2026-10-25 also records the wall clock of the EasySpin call
(`/spec/seconds`, `/curry/seconds` or `/result/seconds`; a second call after
a warm-up, a fit timed once). `scripts/easyspin-timings.py` reads it for the
representative cases of the timing table on the project site
(`comparison/easyspin/timings.json`) and times `easyspyn` on the same inputs;
older cases without it show as "not timed" there until regenerated.

A system listing spin-zero nuclei with one `A` row per nucleus
(`'12C,13C,16O'`) is not a case: EasySpin 6.0.15's `ham_hf` errors on it
(it tests the unprocessed `A` for the full-matrix form after the nuclei were
removed). `easyspyn` handles it; `tests/easyspyn/test_foundation.py` covers it.
