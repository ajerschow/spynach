# Plan

SpynDynamica ports SpinDynamica 3.10.2 to Python: a symbolic core, a numeric engine and a bridge between
them, depending on NumPy, SciPy and SymPy only. It lives in the Spynach repository as a separate import
(`src/spyndynamica`, tests and fixtures in `tests/spyndynamica`) with no dependency on `spynach`. This file
replaces the planning handoff; it keeps its phases and records what was found since.

## Rules

- SpinDynamica is a reference, not the truth. Every foundation result is checked independently as well
  (`tests/spyndynamica/test_reference_invariants.py`). Where the two disagree the port follows the correct result and the
  case goes into `DIVERGENCES.md`.
- Conventions (order, phases, normalisation) follow SpinDynamica even where they are arbitrary, because
  users compare results.
- Algorithms are chosen on their merits. `tests/spyndynamica/fixtures/data/timings.json` is a baseline to beat, not a target,
  and the Mathematica code is not transliterated.
- No SpinDynamica source in the repository. The source is read from `SPINDYNAMICA_HOME` and never written.
  Credit SpinDynamica and its publication wherever the port is described (`LICENSE`, the package README, the guide).
- Symbolic results are checked by their values at fixed parameter points first and by exact matrix
  comparison second. Printed forms are never compared.

## What the handoff got wrong

- SpinDynamica runs without the notebook front end. The fixtures are generated by `wolframscript`.
- Parsing Mathematica expressions with SymPy is not the standard check; values at parameter points are.
  The exact text of matrices is still stored and parses (`tests/spyndynamica/reference.py: exact`).
- Stored outputs of the documentation notebooks are not used. Examples are re-run as case files.

## Conventions pinned by the fixtures

All of these are covered by a fixture and an independent test.

- **Zeeman basis:** `m` runs from `+I` down for every spin, and the first spin changes fastest. For two
  spins-1/2 the order is `αα, βα, αβ, ββ`.
- **Singlet-triplet basis:** `S0, T+1, T0, T-1`, with `S0 = (αβ − βα)/√2`.
- **Tensor operators:** `T(1, ±1) = ∓I±/√2`, `T(1, 0) = Iz`; for a pair `T(0, 0) = −I1·I2/√3` and
  `T(2, 0) = (3 I1z I2z − I1·I2)/√6`; `T(k, −q) = (−1)^q T(k, q)†`; the Racah commutators hold to rank 3.
- **Rotations:** `opR[spins, {β, φ}] = exp(−iβ (Ix cos φ + Iy sin φ))`; Euler angles are zyz,
  `Rz(α) Ry(β) Rz(γ)`; `WignerD[J][{α, β, γ}]` has elements `exp(−i m α) d(β) exp(−i m' γ)` with indices
  from `+J` down, and `Wignerd` equals SymPy's `Rotation.d`. A tensor operator transforms as
  `R T(k, q) R† = Σ T(k, q') D(q', q)`.
- **Spin permutations:** `SpinPermutationOperator[{1, 2, 3}]` moves the state of spin 1 to spin 2.
- **Liouville space:** all named operator bases are orthonormal under `(A|B) = Tr(A†B)`. The default is
  `ShiftAndZOperatorBasis` sorted by coherence order. A superoperator matrix is `S_ij = (B_i | S B_j)`.
  `OperatorAmplitude[a -> b] = (b|a)/(b|b)`.
- **Dynamics:** free evolution is `exp(−iHt) ρ exp(+iHt)`; the Liouvillian is `−i[H, ·] + R`; the
  phenomenological relaxation superoperator is negative semidefinite with rates `1/T1` and `1/T2`.
  `Lindbladian[A, B] ρ = A ρ B − (B A ρ + ρ B A)/2`.
- **Interactions:** the Larmor frequency is `−γ B0`; the dipolar coupling is `−(μ0/4π) γ1 γ2 ħ / r³`;
  the constants are CODATA 2010.

## Design of the symbolic core

- An operator is a map from terms to SymPy coefficients. A term has one single-spin basis label per spin.
- The single-spin basis is Cartesian (`e`, `x`, `y`, `z`) for a spin-1/2 and unity plus the tensor operators
  `T(k, q)` for a higher spin. Both are orthogonal under the trace, so products, adjoints and expansions come
  from exact matrices by projection, computed once per spin quantum number and cached (`_local.py`).
- A rotation acts on the labels: a 3 × 3 rotation of `(x, y, z)` for a spin-1/2, a Wigner matrix per rank for
  the tensor operators. No matrix exponential is taken.
- Matrices are derived from the terms. The algebra never goes through dense symbolic matrices, which the
  planning measurements showed to be too slow.

## Phases

1. **Reference harness.** Done: generator, loader, 976 cases in twenty files, independent checks.
2. **Symbolic core.** Done (`tests/spyndynamica/test_core.py`): spin systems, kets and bras, the Zeeman and
   singlet-triplet bases, product-operator and spherical-tensor algebra, rotations, closed-form evolution for
   commuting spin-1/2 terms, exact and numeric matrices. Pinned by `01_bases`, `02_operators`, `03_rotations`.
3. **Numeric engine with a backend interface.** Done (`tests/spyndynamica/test_liouville_dynamics.py`):
   operator bases, superoperators as sums of sandwiches and dyads of symbolic operators, the phenomenological
   relaxation model, events (`Pulse`, `Delay`, `Repeat`, generator and duration, instantaneous superoperators),
   propagation in Hilbert space or in Liouville space as the events require, transformation amplitudes and
   sampled trajectories. Linear algebra goes through `backend.py`. Pinned by `04_liouville` and
   `05_propagation`. Left out: SpinDynamica's sequence of the shift-and-z basis for spins above 1/2, and
   trajectories as interpolating functions.
4. **Signals and relaxation.** Done (`tests/spyndynamica/test_signals.py`): `signal_1d` by diagonalisation
   of the Hamiltonian or of the Liouvillian and by direct propagation, `Signal` with peaks or samples, line
   broadening, `ft`, the lineshape functions, the isotope table, constants, Larmor frequencies and dipolar
   couplings. Pinned by `06_signals` and `07_tables`. SpinDynamica has no routine for a
   two-dimensional signal; its routines for two-dimensional data came with stage E.
5. **Time dependence and ensembles.** Done (`tests/spyndynamica/test_time_ensembles.py`): time-dependent
   Hamiltonians as events (`TimeDependent`, integrated by the backend, also with relaxation) and in
   `signal_1d`, the COMPUTE method for `Periodic` Hamiltonians, `ensemble_average` with optional threads, the
   tabulated orientation sets and the regular ones, and the spherical tensor conversions and transformation.
   Pinned by `08_time_dependence`. The random orientation sets and process-based parallelism came with
   parity stage A, signals under time-dependent Hamiltonians with relaxation with stage E.
6. **Performance.** Partly done (`tests/spyndynamica/test_performance.py`): matrices are built as sparse
   Kronecker products (`Operator.sparse`), ideal pulses are built numerically from single-spin rotation
   matrices and never expanded symbolically, and `method="sparse"` (automatic from dimension 128) splits every
   sparse Hamiltonian into the blocks it does not connect and diagonalises those. Measured on a chain of
   spins-1/2 with shifts and nearest-neighbour couplings, a pulse and one evolution interval (4 threads):

   | spins | before | dense | sparse blocks |
   |---|---|---|---|
   | 8 | 0.21 s | 0.06 s | 0.04 s |
   | 10 | 7.1 s | 0.76 s | 0.16 s |
   | 12 | not run | not run | 6.0 s |

   Tried and dropped: applying the exponential to the full density matrix with Krylov steps
   (`expm_multiply`), 15 s for 10 spins and 385 s for 12. Open: pulses applied spin by spin without a dense
   unitary (the 12-spin time is mostly two dense products), state-vector and restricted-state-space
   propagation, sparse Liouvillians, a GPU or autodiff backend behind `backend.py`, optimisation routines.
   Direction from the user (2026-11-23): port what SpinDynamica itself contains first, with modest
   optimisations, and leave advanced numerical techniques to Spynach. The open items above that go beyond
   SpinDynamica (state-vector and restricted-space propagation, sparse Liouvillians, GPU, autodiff) are
   shelved; phase 6 is closed as it stands.

   **Parity stage A (the rest of the simulation layer).** Done (`tests/spyndynamica/test_states.py`,
   `test_sequences.py`; `09_states`, `10_sequences`): `thermal_equilibrium`, `polarized`, `singlet_polarized`,
   `secularize`, `thermalize`, `amplitude_table`, `maximize_amplitude`; `PulseSequence` with automatic
   delays, default spins and a phase shift, `ShapedPulse`, `precalculate`, pulse `phase_shift` and
   `amplitude_factor`, `normalization`, `start_time`, coherence-order filtration as an event, `ft2`,
   `random_orientations`, ensemble averages in processes. Left out:
   `AdiabaticPropagationSuperoperator`, which SpinDynamica documents as internal.
   **Stage B (the rest of the quantum-mechanics layer).** Done
   (`tests/spyndynamica/test_bases_operators.py`, `test_filters_bases_geometry.py`; `11_bases_and_operators`,
   `12_bases_filters_geometry`): `angular_momentum_basis`, `eigenbasis` with a target basis,
   `Basis.transformed`, `Ket.magnetic_quantum_numbers`, `Operator.normalized`, `Operator.projection`,
   `shift_superoperator`; tensor operators of any number of spins coupled from left to right (`s.T` with
   `ranks` and `history`, `rank_histories`), the spherical tensor operator basis of any spin system,
   `spherical_component_filter` (for any spins, checked against the integral over the rotation group),
   `define_basis`, `check_basis`, `product_basis`, `define_operator_basis`, `check_operator_basis`,
   `OperatorBasis.reordered` and `.transformed`, `axis_system`, `axes_to_euler`, `rotate_euler` about an axis
   system, `polar_rotation_matrix`, `rotate_polar`, the quaternion routines, `vector_to_polar`,
   `polar_to_vector`, and `distance`, `bond_angle`, `subtended_angle`, `torsion_angle`, `solid_angle`. Four divergences were found (D7 to D10). Left out: incomplete state bases (symbolic
   axes in `axes_to_euler` came with stage E).
   **Stage C (the advanced modules).** Done (`tests/spyndynamica/test_exchange_algebras_groups.py`,
   `test_graphics.py`; `13_polarization_exchange_algebras`, `14_graphics`):
   - *Polarization* (`states.py`): `polarization_level`, `singlet_polarization_level`.
   - *Extended Liouville space* (`exchange.py`): `SiteDependent` operators and superoperators,
     `ExtendedOperatorBasis` with `vector`, `matrix`, `numeric` and `split`, `kinetic_exchange_matrix`,
     `KineticExchange`, and sums of all of these as one matrix. SpinDynamica has the module
     (`QM/ExtendedLiouville.m`) but does not load it, and several of its operations are unfinished; the
     fixtures load it explicitly and pin what works.
   - *Groups and algebras* (`algebras.py`, `groups.py`): `commutant`, `bicommutant`, `lie_algebra`,
     `abelian_subalgebra`, `is_maximal_abelian`, `inaccessible`, with exact linear algebra;
     `permutation_group`, `spin_permutation_group`, `symmetry_adapted_basis` (deterministic, for any
     permutation group of equal spins) and `dual_pairing` (from Schur polynomials, as a dictionary).
   - *Graphics* (`graphics.py`): `Scene` objects of plain arrays with an optional Plotly figure; `ovaloid`,
     `ovaloid_radius`, `axes_3d`, `arrow_3d`, `visualize_euler`, `l_shape`, `rotor`, `sphere_and_axes`, and
     translation and rotation of scenes (SpinDynamica's `RotateEuler` and `RotatePolar` on graphics).
   Two more divergences were found (D11, D12). The simulation routines do not use the extended Liouville
   space, in SpinDynamica or here: a signal under exchange is built from the matrices by hand.
7. **Advanced modules.** Done as parity stage C (above): groups and algebras, polarization, extended
   Liouville space, 3D graphics.
8. **Documentation and browser subset** (parity stage D). Done. The guides are split by theme
   (`docs/spyndynamica.md`, `spyndynamica-simulations.md`, `spyndynamica-symmetry.md`,
   `coming-from-spindynamica.md`), and every exported name appears in one of them
   (`tests/spyndynamica/test_documentation.py`). The browser playground fetches the package with the others
   (`scripts/build_site.py`, `worker.js`), loads SymPy from Pyodide when an example imports `spyndynamica`,
   and has three examples that the tests run natively (`tests/unit/test_playground_examples.py`): INEPT by
   product operators, a strongly coupled pair with `signal_1d`, and two-site exchange in extended Liouville
   space. The three examples were also run in Pyodide 314 (Python 3.14) under Node with the file list of `worker.js`; the
   page itself was not opened in a browser.

9. **The rest of the "not ported" list** (parity stage E). Done (`tests/spyndynamica/test_carousel_relaxation.py`,
   `test_two_dimensional.py`, `test_symbolic_axes.py`; `15_carousel_relaxation`, `16_two_dimensional`,
   `17_symbolic_axes`):
   - *Carousel average*: `signal_1d(..., carousel=True)`, the γ-COMPUTE average over the start of the
     acquisition within the period, in Hilbert space and in Liouville space. Checked against the explicit
     average of step-by-step signals with shifted Hamiltonians and against Bessel-function sidebands.
   - *Signals under time-dependent Hamiltonians with relaxation*: the step-by-step method and COMPUTE in
     Liouville space, with `background` relaxation; an acquisition that starts later (`start`) now evolves
     under the time-dependent generator from time zero, also for COMPUTE.
   - *Two-dimensional data*: `ft2` with options per dimension, `frequency_axis`, `reflect_spectrum`,
     `combine_complementary_spectra`, `combine_data`. SpinDynamica has no two-dimensional signal routine.
   - *Symbolic axes*: `axis_system` and `axes_to_euler` with symbols, valid in the first quadrant as in
     SpinDynamica.
   - *Relaxation theory*: nothing to port; SpinDynamica has the phenomenological model only.

   Two more divergences were found: D13 (the COMPUTE method is normalised differently from the other
   methods) and D14 (embedded frequency coordinates of two-dimensional data).
10. **The rest of the list** (parity stage F). Done (`tests/spyndynamica/test_analysis_adiabatic.py`;
   `18_analysis_adiabatic`, `19_trajectory_functions`, `20_shift_and_z_order`; guide
   `docs/spyndynamica-adiabatic.md`):
   - *Trajectory functions* (`dynamics.py`): `trajectory_function`, `operator_trajectory_function`, exact at
     every time.
   - *Adiabatic events* (`dynamics.py`, `backend.py`): `Adiabatic(generator, duration, sampling,
     delete_coherences)`, with eigenstates followed by overlap and parallel transport and the dynamic phase
     from a spline integral. Checked against the integrated equation of motion for slower and slower sweeps.
   - *Analysis* (`analysis.py`): `amplitude_bounds`, `correlated_eigensystems`.
   - *Embedded coordinates* (`signals.py`): `embed_coordinates`, `split_coordinates`, `ft_coordinates`.
   - *Nothing to port:* incomplete state bases (they do not work in SpinDynamica).
   - *Not portable:* the sequence of the shift-and-z basis within one coherence order for spins above 1/2
     (Mathematica's sorting of expressions); the port's basis is checked to hold the same operators in every
     coherence order.

   One more divergence was found: D15 (`CorrelatedEigensystems` with a parameter always fails).

11. **Cell view of the playground.** Done. Examples written in cells (`# %% title`) run cell by cell in one
   namespace of the worker, with the value of the last line of a cell typeset by KaTeX
   (`assets/playground-cells.js`, the cell mode of the runner in `worker.js`, `try.html`), and six symbolic
   examples use it: COSY, the singlet-triplet basis, dipolar relaxation from double commutators, an
   amplitude against a flip angle, bounds on a transfer, the average Hamiltonian of WAHUHA. Tested natively
   (`tests/unit/test_playground_cells.py`, `test_playground_runner.py`), in Pyodide under Node
   (`scripts/pyodide-examples.mjs`, which now loads SymPy), and the LaTeX of every cell with KaTeX under
   Node. Not done: the page in a browser (see the report of that session). JupyterLite for whole guides was
   considered and left for later.
